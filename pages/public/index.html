About Us

Myridia.com is an IT company. We build in-house software for digital life that matters: tools that protect words, files and secrets.

Our mission is to help secure the world. We believe strong encryption should be simple, available to everyone and actually used — not reserved for the paranoid few. Every message, every document, every identity deserves a strong layer of protection.

Our products, such as the ct text encryption tool, put military-grade AES-128-GCM encryption into everyday tools. Text, CLI, images. So strong security stops being a specialist task and becomes the default.
